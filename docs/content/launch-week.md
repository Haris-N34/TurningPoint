# Launch Week content notes

Playable at `/play/launch-week`. Fifteen nodes follow the PRD acts. Node 13 combines market day with the feedback decision; node 14 opens the real-life checkpoint; node 15 ends the run. Eligible decisions are 3, 6, 9, 10 and 12. All paths visit the same situations, with different resource consequences and explanations. No route is labelled correct.

The opening grant is fixed at $5,000 so the sponsor's $3,000 withdrawal represents 60% of that grant. It is a fixed delta, not 60% of the unspent balance. Team size varies from 6 to 10. The disruption is seeded by the engine; the first pool entry is the sponsor, but random candidate runs can receive any of the three disruptions. A demo using a fixed seed should verify its selected disruption before presenting.

The 15-minute estimate budgets about 5 minutes for reading, 3 minutes for ten decisions, 2.5 to 3.75 minutes for decision checkpoints, and 4 minutes for the real-life answer and follow-up. Early submissions can shorten a run. Automated playthroughs use early submissions and do not establish a human completion time. C2.5 stays open pending a timed first-user run of 12 to 18 minutes.

Illustration keys and alt text are added under #13. No missing image paths are introduced by this content task.
